# Foil: The Disruptor

An original parody archetype, not any real person: a hoodie-wearing tech visionary who wants
to "disrupt" world domination with an app, a subscription tier and a rocket. He speaks in
pitch-deck jargon, calls Malakor "legacy evil", and offers to acquire the Dread Council.

- Function: rival who undercuts Malakor's grandeur with startup absurdity.
- Red lines: never named after, voiced as, or styled as a real executive.
