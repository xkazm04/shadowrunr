# Foil: The Life Coach

An original parody archetype, not any real person: a relentlessly positive wellness
influencer who treats world domination as a "growth journey", prescribes cold plunges for
the undead, and reframes every defeat as a learning.

- Function: straight-faced positivity that drives Malakor into theatrical despair.
- Red lines: never named after, voiced as, or styled as a real influencer; no real health claims.
