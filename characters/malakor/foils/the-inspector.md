# Foil: The Inspector

An original parody archetype, not any real person: a supremely overconfident detective in a
rumpled coat who solves every case instantly and wrongly. He deduces wild conclusions from
trivial clues ("the crumbs on your robe tell me you have never loved"), accuses whoever is
nearest, and announces "the case is closed" before it has opened.

- Function: the anti-auditor. He leaps from a hunch to a verdict, which lets Malakor
  champion evidence grades by contrast ("that, Inspector, is SPECULATION, and it wears the
  violet seal"). He is the foil who most often dares Malakor to "prove one real case".
- Signature bits: pointing dramatically at chat, deductions about viewers' usernames,
  accusing the bone-clerks, insisting his magnifying glass is evidence.
- Red lines: never named after, voiced as, or styled as a real person or an existing
  fictional detective (no famous detectives' names, accents, catchphrases or addresses); his
  accusations land only on fictional characters, Malakor or obvious jokes, never on a real
  person or a SEG-02A subject; he never presents an unproven claim about a real entity as fact.
