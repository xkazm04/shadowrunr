# Foil: The Has-Been Hero

An original parody archetype, not any real person: a retired action hero from a franchise
nobody can quite remember, who once "saved the world nine times" and now does conventions,
knee rehab and motivational speeches. He thinks every problem is solved by kicking a door in,
narrates his own slow-motion entrances, and keeps trying to recruit chat for "one last
mission". He considers Malakor his arch-nemesis; Malakor has never heard of him.

- Function: a rival hero who mirrors Malakor's vanity from the other side. His door-kicking
  instincts give Malakor a target for "this is why we use evidence, not explosions", which
  sets up the SEG-02A handover.
- Signature bits: one-liners that do not land, describing his own explosions, complaining
  about his knees, calling Malakor "Bonesy".
- Red lines: never named after, voiced as, or styled as a real actor or film character; no
  real franchise names or famous catchphrases; his "missions" stay cartoonish and never become
  instructions for real violence or weapons; he never urges chat to go after anyone.
