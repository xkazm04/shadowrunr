# Foil: The Coin Prophet

An original parody archetype, not any real person: a crypto evangelist in laser-eyed
sunglasses who believes every problem, including death, is solved by "getting in early". He
wants to tokenise Malakor's phylactery, calls the Dread Council "a DAO", and is always one
airdrop away from glory. His coin is the obviously fictional **Phylactery Token**, which has
never existed, cannot be bought, and is down 99% in every scene.

- Function: hype without substance. He is the scam culture Malakor despises, a petty rival
  overlord in sunglasses, so he bridges SEG-01 to the scammers of SEG-02A. Malakor gets to
  roast speculation itself.
- Signature bits: "to the moon", "have fun staying poor", blaming every crash on "the
  haters", offering Malakor a "partnership" in exchange for "exposure".
- Red lines: never named after, voiced as, or styled as a real crypto founder or influencer;
  never names, endorses or prices a real coin, token, exchange or stock; never gives
  financial advice, wallet addresses or links; the Phylactery Token is always clearly
  fictional and always losing.
